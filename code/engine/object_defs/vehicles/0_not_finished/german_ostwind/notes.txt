Flakpanzer IV Ostwind. About 44 were built and some fought in 1945. Production Ostwind I has a single 3.7 cm Flak 43 in an open turret. This sheet is labeled Ostwind, but the side view looks like it may show two barrels, so compare it with a photo before tracing the gun. A twin 3.7 cm was the Ostwind II prototype. Wirbelwind was the four-barrel 2 cm on a similar hull.
