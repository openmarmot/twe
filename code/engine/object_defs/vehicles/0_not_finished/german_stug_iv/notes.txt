StuG IV

A late StuG IV gets the remote roof MG 34 (Rundumsfeuer), the same mount as the Hetzer. The loader aims it through a periscope and fires it from inside. It is an MG 34, not an MG 42.

From about the summer of 1944 this mount replaced the shield over the loader's hatch. An early StuG IV still has that exposed shield gun.

The StuG III G already in the game is the earlier fit: coaxial MG 34, no roof mount. Do not copy that onto a 1944-45 StuG IV.

The Jagdpanzer IV was tried with this roof mount in March and April 1944 and it was dropped. Issued Jagdpanzer IVs have a covered MG port in the front plate.
