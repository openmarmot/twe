BM-13N Katyusha on a ZiS-151. This sheet is the 1947 truck, not the wartime mount. The 16-rail 132 mm launcher is the part to use. In 1945 the same launcher usually sat on a Studebaker US6, and no top view of that truck turned up. Width on the sheet is given as about 2.3 m.
