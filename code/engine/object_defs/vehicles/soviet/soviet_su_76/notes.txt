Soviet SU 76

Late-war SU-76 (the main production SU-76M from mid-1943 onward) had an overall width of 2714 mm (2.714 m). Some sources round to 2.7 m or list 2720–2740 mm for earlier variants, but 2714 mm is the consistent figure for the SU-76M.At your 8 pixels per foot scale:
