Single MG 151/20 on an improvised ground mount.

Luftwaffe ground troops used surplus aircraft guns this way late in
the war, mostly for airfield and local object defense. A pipe or logs
set in the ground, sometimes a crude shield. Single and twin mounts
show up in photos. The triple Drilling was the systematic mount.

This object is the 20mm gun. The 15mm came first and was largely
dropped once 15mm ammunition ran down. The sprite is the sMG42.
Armor is zero until a shield is drawn.

Crew push. 75 kg, speed cap 85. Full traverse. The gun starts on
Minengeschoss. Stored belts are in inventory, same as the sMG42.
