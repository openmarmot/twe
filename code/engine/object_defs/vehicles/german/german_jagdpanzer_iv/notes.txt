Jagdpanzer IV (Sd.Kfz. 162)

Not the Panzer IV/70. That one has the 7.5 cm Pak 42 L/70 and is a separate vehicle.

-- ref ---

https://web.archive.org/web/20100313170412/http://afvdb.50megs.com/germany/jagdpz4.html

Vomag, January-November 1944, about 769. Crew of 4.
Gun is the 7.5 cm Pak 39 L/48 (same rounds as the KwK 40 L/48).
Traverse 10 degrees left and right, handwheel. Sight Sfl.ZF 1a.
Stowage 79 rounds. Engine Maybach HL 120 TRM, 300 PS, gasoline.
Road speed 40 km/h. Combat weight about 24 tonnes. Fuel 470 liters
(the game tank uses the shared 114, same as the Panzer IV).

Two game vehicles share this art.

german_jagdpanzer_iv is January-April 1944, about chassis 320001-320300.
german_jagdpanzer_iv_late is from chassis 320301 (May 1944). Chamberlain
and Doyle: the upper hull front and the superstructure front went to
80 mm, and the superstructure sides went from 30 mm to 40 mm. The lower
nose stayed 50 mm. Weight is the heavy end of the published 24-25 tonnes.

The May vehicles usually left the factory without a muzzle brake. Both
variants use the same gun sprite, which still has the brake. Dust from
the brake is not modeled. Speed, rack, crew, and the roof MG are the same.
Return rollers dropping from four to three, and steel-rimmed wheels, are
not modeled. The 45 degree upper hull front (60 mm, then 80 mm) sits
between the nose and the casemate. Low hits use the 50 mm nose.
